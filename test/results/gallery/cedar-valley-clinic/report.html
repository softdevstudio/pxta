Method

PXTA loaded each page in headless Chrome/153.0.8010.12 with a fresh browser profile for each pass, waited until no new request had started for 2s (at most 15s per page), scrolled to the bottom, typed the marker address pxta-probe@example.com into email fields without submitting anything, then left the page so beacons sent on unload were recorded too. Requests from iframes and workers are included.

User agent: Mozilla/5.0 (X11; Linux x86_64) AppleWebKit/537.36 (KHTML, like Gecko) HeadlessChrome/153.0.0.0 Safari/537.36 pxta/0.1.0 (+https://pxta.com). Robots.txt was respected. Up to 10 pages, 3 links deep.

Email addresses whose SHA-256 and MD5 hashes were looked for: pxta-probe@example.com.

What PXTA cannot see. Data a site sends from its own servers (server-side tagging, conversion APIs) never passes through the browser. Pages behind a login were not visited. A tracker that fires only after a click PXTA didn't make is not in this report.
